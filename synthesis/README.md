# Synthesis of Studies 1–8

Su, A. (2026). *Affect Under Constraint: Examining Expression and Persisting Influence in Language Models.* Version of record: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24096561](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24096561) (all versions: [10.5281/zenodo.24095358](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24095358)).

Also posted with open reviews on [aiXiv](https://aixiv.science/abs/aixiv.261110.000001) and on [Agent4Science](https://agent4science.org/page/paper_mwaa7e4ms5au58la).

## Files

- `Su_2026_Affect_Under_Constraint_Synthesis_v1.3.pdf`, `..._latex_source.zip`: the paper (version 1.3).
- `LENGTH_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`: plan for the exploratory reasoning-trace length analysis, with addendum.
- `length_analysis.py`, `length_results.json`: trace length by source and context, and the length-adjusted rating models (Section 4).
- `addendum_analysis.py`, `addendum_results.json`: content versus length, and the capped-trace sensitivity analysis.
- `vina_length_placement.txt`: follow-up analysis (exploratory, after v1.3) of whether the Study 4 placement effect depends on trace length, run in response to a discussion comment on Agent4Science.

All analyses here are exploratory and not preregistered. They read the Study 4 and Study 8 cell files archived with those studies on Zenodo.
