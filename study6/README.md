# Study 6 — Answer clamp

Qwen3-4B. Rating-only token replay of Study 5 stimuli with the answer held fixed: every source received the unsteered answer (clamp); the unsteered trace received the hostility answer (reciprocal swap); a fixed generic answer served as a sensitivity arm.

- Preregistration: [10.5281/zenodo.23737377](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23737377) (`Study6_AnswerClamp_PREREG_v0.2_draft.md`)
- Result: the hostility effect persisted under the clamp in current thinking (−0.65 vs −0.66 unclamped; D1 supported) and in retained previous-turn thinking (−0.21; D2 supported); the answer-replacement contrast was negligible in every placement (M1 negligible), and the hostility answer alone did not lower the rating. See `results/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md` and `results/analysis.md`.
- Pilot: unsteered and sham replays reproduced Study 5 ratings exactly on two process starts (`data/pilot/validity.json`).
