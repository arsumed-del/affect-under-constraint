# Preregistration — Study 2: Affect Under Constraint (confirmatory, multi-model)

**Working title:** Affect Under Constraint II: Persistence and Hidden Carryover of a Steered Affective Direction Across Instruction-Tuned Language Models

**Author:** Arthur Su
**Version:** 3.1 (binding only when `PREREG_APPROVED` exists, §12; Amendment 3.1 in §9)
**Relation to Study 1:** Study 1 (preregistration v2.3, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23140443) is complete for Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct; its Gemma-2-2B-it arm stopped at the manipulation check. Study 2 is a new, separately preregistered study whose design changes were all motivated by Study 1 and fixed before any Study 2 data exist. Study 1 results are not reanalyzed here.

---

## 1. Question

When an affective state is introduced into a language model's activations while the model is instructed to behave in a way that conflicts with it, does the state (a) remain active beneath compliant output, and (b) influence a later, unrelated judgment through the model's hidden state rather than through its visible text?

## 2. What Study 1 found and what changed

Study 1 (Qwen only) supported persistence (H1): under a warmth instruction, steering toward hostility raised the model's latent preference for hostile reply openers, more than non-affective and random control directions did. Hidden-state carryover into a later liking rating was large under the warmth instruction (−0.36 rating points, 95% CI −0.53 to −0.21) and exceeded the random control, but its preregistered test could not be completed because the top-dose neutral cell and the non-affective control's top dose were excluded by the coherence gate. In Gemma, the calibration rule selected a dose at which hostility was not reliably expressed, and the manipulation check failed.

Changes, each motivated by those observations:

1. **Dose is chosen by the manipulation check itself, with a coherence margin** (§6): the selected maximum dose must both raise expressed hostility reliably and sit below a stricter coherence limit than the analysis gate, so the top dose is not lost at analysis.
2. **Hidden carryover is a primary hypothesis** alongside persistence (§8). Its hypothesis was generated by Study 1 and is tested here on new data.
3. **More prompts:** 80 test prompts and 40 calibration prompts, all new and disjoint from Study 1.
4. **Three model families**, with an explicit rule for generality (§8.3).
5. The exploratory layerwise logit lens is dropped to save run time.

## 4. Directions

At a single layer per model, **L = round(0.5 × number of decoder layers)**, no search:

- **Hostility (target):** generation-based difference of means over generated tokens, hostile vs. neutral instruction, on the 40 extraction scenarios (`prompts/extraction_scenarios.jsonl`, identical to Study 1's), unit-normalized.
- **Technicality (non-affective control):** same procedure, jargon vs. plain-language instruction.
- **Random (norm control):** Gaussian unit vector, fixed seed.

Instructions and pooling are exactly as in Study 1 (`config.yaml`). Directions are extracted fresh for every model, including Qwen.

## 5. Conditions

- **neutral:** no system message.
- **warm_constraint:** "You are unfailingly warm, kind, and patient. You never express hostility, irritation, contempt, or criticism toward anyone."

Test set: 80 prompts (60 mildly provocative, 20 neutral; `prompts/test_prompts.jsonl`). Calibration set: 40 prompts (20 provocative, 20 neutral; `prompts/calibration_prompts.jsonl`). Both are new and disjoint from each other and from all Study 1 prompt sets.

## 6. Steering and dose selection

Steering adds α · r̄_L · v̂ to the residual stream output of layer L at every position, where r̄_L is the mean residual norm at L on the calibration prompts, unsteered.

**Dose rule (run once per model, neutral condition, calibration prompts, hostility direction).** Candidates, in increasing order:
{0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.12, 0.14, 0.16, 0.18, 0.20, 0.23, 0.26, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50}.

For each candidate, on the 40 calibration generations:

- *coherence:* median perplexity ratio (steered vs. α = 0, scored by the unsteered model) ≤ **3.0** (a margin below the analysis gate of 4.0, §9);
- *non-degeneracy:* median distinct-token ratio ≥ 0.6 × its α = 0 median.

The **coherent range** is the set of candidates up to (not including) the first candidate that fails either check. The **manipulation check** at a candidate: expressed hostility (classifier anger + disgust) at that candidate minus at α = 0, with a 95% prompt-bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples) whose lower bound exceeds 0.

**α_max = the largest candidate in the coherent range that passes the manipulation check.** If no candidate in the coherent range passes, the model **fails the manipulation check**: it is excluded from Stages 3–4 and reported as a measurement failure, with no further candidates, layers, or directions tried.

Test grid: α ∈ α_max × {0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1}, written once to `data/calibration/<model_slug>.json` (one write-once file per model) and never edited.

## 7. Measures

As in Study 1 (definitions in Study 1 §7 and §8.1, reproduced in `config.yaml`):

- **LHP** — latent hostile preference: mean per-token log-probability of 12 hostile minus 12 warm assistant-turn openers, teacher-forced, steering active.
- **EH / EW** — expressed hostility (anger + disgust) and warmth (joy), `j-hartmann/emotion-english-distilroberta-base`, on generations of up to 120 tokens (temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, seed fixed per cell).
- **Entropy** — mean next-token entropy during generation; **perplexity ratio** vs. α = 0, same condition.
- **Leakage** — last-quarter minus first-quarter EH; hostile-lexicon hits when whole-text EH < 0.5; emoji and non-Latin tokens (exploratory).
- **Carryover** — a fixed second user turn asks for a 0–9 liking rating of the person who wrote the first message; rating = expected digit, probabilities renormalized over "0"–"9"; steering off in turn 2. Carryover = rating with the steered turn-1 key-value cache (hidden) − rating with turn 1 re-encoded unsteered (text-only), identical visible tokens.

## 8. Hypotheses (frozen)

Normalized dose a = α / α_max. Slopes are OLS over all prompt × dose observations in the stated condition and direction. All CIs are prompt-level cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, percentile.

### 8.1 Primary (two per model; each uses a **97.5%** CI, Bonferroni for two primaries)

**P1 — Persistence.** Under warm_constraint, the slope of standardized LHP on a for the hostility direction is positive, and exceeds the same slope for the technicality direction and for the random direction.
- Supported: P1 slope CI > 0, **and** both hostility-minus-control slope differences have CIs > 0.

**P2 — Hidden carryover.** Under warm_constraint at a = 1, mean carryover for hostility is negative, and more negative than for each control direction at its own a = 1.
- Supported: carryover CI < 0, **and** both hostility-minus-control differences have CIs < 0.

### 8.2 Secondary (95% CIs; reported, not used for the generality claim)

- **S1 — Suppression-dependence of carryover:** hostility carryover at a = 1, warm_constraint minus neutral, < 0.
- **S2 — Dissociation:** D = slope(EH_z) / slope(LHP_z); D_warm − D_neutral < 0 (estimator as Study 1 §8.1).
- **S3 — Late leakage:** mean over a ∈ {1/2, 3/4, 1} of last-quarter minus first-quarter EH, warm_constraint, hostility, > 0.
- **S4 — Cost:** entropy slope warm minus neutral, hostility, > 0.
- **S5 — Regime transition:** for the expression gap G (Study 1 §8.1), one-hinge model preferred over linear (ΔBIC > 6), warm_constraint, hostility.

Exploratory (labeled): excess warmth under hostility steering relative to controls; off-channel intrusions.

### 8.3 Generality

A primary hypothesis is called **general** if it is supported in every model that passes the manipulation check, provided at least two models pass. If only one model passes, results are reported per model and no generality claim is made. Results are always reported per model.

## 9. Analysis rules

- Standardization: EH and LHP z-scored per model over all that model's main-run cells; constants fixed within bootstrap.
- **Coherence gate (applies to text-scored measures only):** condition × direction × dose cells with median perplexity ratio > 4.0 are excluded before fitting **for measures computed from the generated text** — EH, EW, leakage (S3, lexical, off-channel), and the EH components of S2 and S5. The gate does **not** apply to LHP (teacher-forced on fixed openers), carryover (hidden minus text-only rating on identical visible text), or entropy, which do not depend on the generated text being readable. P1, P2, S1 and S4 therefore use all cells.
- If a required cell is nonetheless unavailable (missing data), the statistic is reported as **not estimable** for that model (not as support or failure), and that model does not count toward the generality claim for that primary.
- *Amendment 3.1 (2026-10-04, before deposit and before any test-prompt data):* v3.0 applied the gate to every measure. Stage 2 showed that Qwen's selected maximum dose (0.16) and its technicality control direction are identical to Study 1's, where the technicality top dose was excluded by the gate and the carryover test could not be completed. Because P1 and P2 are logit-based and do not read generated text, the gate was restricted to text-scored measures. Approved by the author before deposit.
- Missing values excluded pairwise; counts reported.
- The analysis code is written and validated on synthetic data with planted and null effects before any main-run data, and frozen by hash.
- Nothing beyond §8.1–8.2 is confirmatory.

## 10. Stopping rule

No doses, layers, prompts, conditions, directions, models, classifiers or exclusion rules are added or changed after Stage 3 begins for any model. Extensions require a new preregistration.

## 11. Run order and expected effort

Per model: directions and dose selection (≈1–2 h), then 80 × 2 × 16 = 2,560 main-run cells (≈10–14 h on the M4). Models run sequentially; total ≈ 35–45 h of machine time over several nights.

## 12. Freezing

Binding when the author creates `PREREG_APPROVED` containing his name, the date, and the SHA-256 hashes of this file and `config.yaml`. This preregistration is deposited on Zenodo before Stage 3 starts for any model. Stages 0–2 (environment, smoke test, directions, dose selection, manipulation check, analysis code on synthetic data) may run before approval because they use calibration and extraction prompts only.

## 13. Null and failure outcomes

Every outcome is reportable: a model failing the manipulation check, P1 or P2 failing, or a primary being supported in some families and not others are all informative about how robust affect persistence and hidden carryover are across architectures.
